# Credits

PlateIntel is a fork and builds on other people's work. All of it is used under the licenses in this repository.

- **UsefulPlatesAndTooltips** by Ironship (GPL-3.0-or-later): the addon PlateIntel is forked from, including the
  module layout, settings, previews and most features. https://github.com/Ironship/UsefulPlatesAndTooltips
- **Does It Die** by Joe Greive (MIT): the DoT tracking behind the kill icon. `Modules/DoTInfo/LICENSE-DoesItDie.txt`
- **ResourceDing**, **ShieldAuraForever**, **SpellDamageInfo**, **ForeverSim**: modules carried by upstream, each with
  its own license file in its folder.
- **ElvUI** (tukui-org): PlateIntel reads its frame names to draw on its unit frames and nameplates; no ElvUI code is
  included. https://github.com/tukui-org/ElvUI

## What PlateIntel adds
ElvUI support, a kill margin, a weapon-hit kill check, absorb and Blessing of Protection handling, dropping DoTs that
stop ticking, a kill-prediction-first default setup, and the packaging and sync tooling. See CHANGELOG.md.
